Overview

Sope is a Microsoft 365 and SharePoint consultancy based in Melbourne. I led the visual redesign and rebuild of their marketing website, translating the studio's new brand system into a clearer, more confident web presence for a technical, B2B audience.

The brief covered homepage, service pages and case-study templates, working closely with the leadership team to keep the tone approachable without losing technical credibility.
