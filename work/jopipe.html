Overview

JoPipe is a community app that helps people find and join small interest-based groups nearby — book clubs, running crews, hobby meet-ups — rather than broad, noisy social feeds.

I designed the discovery, group and event flows, focusing on making a brand-new group feel welcoming and low-pressure to join.
