Overview

An iOS-only ticketing platform for small stand-up comedy gigs in pubs and clubs across the UK, covering both sides of the market: audience members buying tickets, and venue or club managers selling them.

Beyond ticketing, the app lets audiences rate and react to shows they've attended, giving comedians and venues lightweight feedback after every gig.
