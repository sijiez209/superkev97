Overview

Dahua Technology Australia partnered with the Starlight Children's Foundation, which brings fun and joy to kids facing serious illness, to run a donation campaign giving back to the community.

The design follows Starlight's own guidelines — yellow and purple as the primary colours, with a five-pointed star as the central motif.
