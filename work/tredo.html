Overview

Tredo is a mobile marketplace concept built for GPost's community-outreach initiative, letting people trade goods, services and favours within their local network.

I designed the end-to-end mobile experience — listing an item, browsing nearby offers, and messaging to close a trade — keeping the flow simple enough for first-time smartphone users.
